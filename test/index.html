What changed since the last walk — brief the team, do not re-report

  • Supervisors have no site. Accounts are created with no site question; a validator assigns them to job references, and that is their whole scope. "You're not assigned to a site yet" no longer exists.
  • Units and stages are Super Admin masters. Units (SQM, NOS, Shift) must exist before Admin 1 can add a line item — the unit is a dropdown now.
  • Teams are split per line item. Admin 2 allocates the WO's teams across line items; each line's target uses its own teams.
  • The checker sees the package. Approvals shows line items, rates (old → new), and stages — read them aloud before approving.
  • JRs carry a Site (Pellet Plant / Blast Furnace) — chosen at issue, shown in the register, never part of the number.

How the data flows now

ADM909Accounts, units, stages Role templates auto-grant permissions. Supervisors are created like anyone else — no site. The units master and the stage catalogue (with display order) are Super Admin's; nobody types a unit or a stage name downstream.
ADM101New Job Data → a draft package Location → Client → Vertical → the pack (client+location+code, live preview) → plant sites under the location. The WO's line items carry SrvLnNo-Service# and a unit from the dropdown; rates and stage maps stage into the draft. Submit stays disabled until the package can price work.
ADM909Approvals — the checker sees everything The review pane shows the header diff (with real names, not ids), the line items added, every staged rate as old → new against the last approved rate, and the stage split. Return sends it back editable; approve makes it all live at once.
ADM201People, measuring stick, and the split Points per designation (the points table IS the designation list), std teams picked from a dropdown, the effective-dated team count — and now the allocation: each line item gets its share of the WO's teams, the form pre-fills the remainder, and the sum can never exceed the count. Each line's Fixed Target = its own teams × per-day × rate.
VAL201JR issued — with a Site — and a supervisor assigned The JR takes a plant site at issue (required once the location has sites). Then the validator assigns a supervisor from their own pool, effective-dated and append-only — a later row hands the JR over from its date, and the month shows both supervisors' slices.
SUP301Entry — scoped by assignment, crew per line A supervisor sees exactly the open JRs assigned to them today. One line item: the day's crew attributes automatically. Two or more: each line gets its crew pickers, everyone must be placed, and one person cannot be on two lines — refused by name.
VAL201Validation — money is born per line Approve computes per line item: WDV (qty × rate × weightage — or qty × rate where no stages are mapped), that line's prorated target from ITS crew and ITS teams, efficiency, points. Return moves nothing; reject is dead.
Closure → Billed → Dashboard Unchanged: measured value at closure = Actual (Unbilled); billed only on fully-closed JRs, corrections stack; the five-row report traces every rupee, and the JR register now groups by Site.
