What this simulation is

A physics-accurate simulation of SPS's inspection machines in Gazebo (Harmonic), flown on the real ArduPilot autopilot software: the Gen5 drone that presses its ultrasonic probe against a 45 m chimney, and the magnetic crawler rovers that stick to steel pipes — including fully upside down. Every calibration traces to a measurement made on the real hardware (luggage scale, kitchen scale, tape measure, KDE thrust bench).

Honest lines, if the room asks

  • Nothing can break. The engine models rigid bodies — impacts stop the aircraft, they cannot damage it. An intact drone after a strike is not a survivability claim.
  • The oil-film result: 1 mm of oil costs the OMNI rover 31 % of grip — leaving 13× its weight. No realistic film detaches it.
  • The old single-magnet plan (20 kgf) cannot hang inverted at all — physics predicted the failure, the sim reproduced it. That is why the two-magnet design exists.

The machines

Gen5 drone FULLY PROVEN

12.000 kg from CAD with battery + 550 g Tritex UT kit · real 18.5″ tri-blade props · 819 mm motor spacing (tape-measured) · thrust from the KDE bench table. Flies ArduPilot SITL: hover, wind, RC, and the full chimney-touch mission all validated. Gel-fed probe disc leads by design; static plastic stabilizer below it.

OMNI rover FULLY PROVEN

7.0 kg · 120 kgf magnetic front wheel + 50–60 kgf rear arc (5 magnets), both luggage-scale measured. Holds and crawls on flat and Ø500 pipe, hangs fully inverted (level within 3.75°). The oil-film decrement study is built on it.

Dyna Flux rover MECHANISM DEMO MAGNETS MEASURED

14.0 kg · 20–22 kgf per wheel (measured) · suspended-magnet wheels: a servo-held magnet inside an aluminum shell, swung up to release. Crawls around a pipe, holds inverted, skid-steers (tank turn). Attached 360° pipe turns: real machine does it, sim work in progress.

Orbit 1.1 rover MECHANISM DEMO

18.0 kg (wheels 1.5 kg each, kitchen-scale measured) · 40 kgf magnet ring per wheel · wheels pivot 0↔90° (2 motors each) to orbit the pipe: along, then around, raster-scanning. Pivot mechanism validated; shown as a model + mechanism at the review.

Demo 1 — RC flight, step by step

Setup from cold: ~3 minutes. Three terminals. Written from the rehearsal; the warnings are things that actually happened.

Before anyone is watching: transmitter dongle in USB · transmitter switched ON · throttle stick fully DOWN (in rehearsal it sat near full and would have blocked arming — check every time).
1
Terminal 1 — start the simulator 
cd ~/gz_ws/src/GazebooAssets
export GZ_SIM_RESOURCE_PATH="$PWD/Models/converted:$PWD/Gen5Flight/models:$PWD/InspectionYard/exports:$GZ_SIM_RESOURCE_PATH"
export GZ_SIM_SYSTEM_PLUGIN_PATH="$HOME/gz_ws/build:$GZ_SIM_SYSTEM_PLUGIN_PATH"
gz sim -r Gen5Flight/worlds/gen5_plant.sdf

Wait until the drone is visible on the ground.

2
Terminal 2 — start the autopilot
Use the launcher script — never sim_vehicle.py directly. The script sets the flag without which the transmitter is silently invisible (sticks do nothing, no error).
~/gz_ws/src/GazebooAssets/Gen5Flight/scripts/start_sitl.sh

Wait for Received ... parameters and a STABILIZE> prompt.

3
Terminal 3 — connect the transmitter 
cd ~/gz_ws/src/GazebooAssets
SDL_LINUX_JOYSTICK_CLASSIC=1 python3 Gen5Flight/scripts/rc_bridge.py --check

Two columns of matching numbers appear. Move every stick once and watch all four respond — a stick that has never moved reports itself centred no matter where it is. Leave this window running for the whole flight; closing it releases the controls.

Wind control

Works in the main demo world (gen5_plant.sdf). Wind ramps in over ~3 s — it arrives like weather, not a switch. It is a world property: it does not follow the drone's nose.

Point and click

cd ~/gz_ws/src/GazebooAssets
python3 Gen5Flight/scripts/wind_panel.py

Tick Enable wind → pick where it comes FROM → speed slider → Apply. Tick Gusting (e.g. ±3 m/s every 8 s). The red STOP WIND button is the only correct off switch. Closing the window does not stop a steady wind.

Command line (same mechanism)

python3 Gen5Flight/scripts/set_wind.py --from-heading 0 --speed 5
python3 Gen5Flight/scripts/set_wind.py --from-heading 0 --speed 5 --gust 3 --period 8
python3 Gen5Flight/scripts/set_wind.py --off

Worth knowing before the room asks

  • The drone will not visibly crash or break — impacts stop it dead; nothing fractures in any physics engine. Heavier aircraft look more survivable than reality; the report states this.
  • Arming and modes are typed, never flicked — the transmitter has no switches.
  • If the machine was ever rebuilt: the file /etc/udev/rules.d/99-rc-joystick.rules must exist or the transmitter is invisible.
